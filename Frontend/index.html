FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is BIS-Sahayak? +
BIS-Sahayak is an intelligent platform designed to help users discover, understand and navigate BIS standards, compliance requirements and certification information.
How does the BIS-Sahayak AI chatbot work? +
The assistant is designed to retrieve relevant BIS information from its knowledge base and present it in a clear, contextual way.
How can I find the right BIS standard for my product? +
Describe your product and intended use. The Standard Finder can guide you toward potentially relevant BIS standards.
Can BIS-Sahayak help me understand compliance requirements? +
Yes. The Compliance Checker is designed to organize applicable requirements and present the steps in a clearer format.
Does BIS-Sahayak support multiple languages? +
The interface is designed with multilingual access in mind, starting with English, Hindi and Bengali.
How does the certification roadmap help me? +
The roadmap asks relevant questions about your product and creates a structured view of the possible certification journey.
